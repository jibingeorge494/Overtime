Rules in use
  • Same shift. Come back within 12 hours of your first Start and it counts as the same shift. The leave before it is cancelled, so the other plant and lunch drives stay paid.
  • New day. After more than 12 hours it is a new day and your last leave stands. A new shift can start no earlier than 4 hours before your usual start.
  • Pay week. Runs Sunday to Saturday. A new week starts at midnight going into Sunday, and the finished week moves to History. A shift that starts Saturday and runs past midnight stays on Saturday.
  • Sunday. First day of the week. Manual entry only. No Start or End.
  • Clock in rounds up to the next 15 minutes. 6:48 counts as 7:00.
  • Clock out rounds down to the previous 15 minutes. 3:50 counts as 3:45.
  • Lunch. 30 minutes unpaid, taken off once a day.
  • Each day. 8 standard hours, 12 at most. Shown as 8 + extra.
  • Each week. 40 standard, then overtime. Aim for 40 + 18, never past 40 + 20.
